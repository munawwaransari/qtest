- 2
"secretly" is to "openly" as "silently" is to 
[A] Scarcely
[B] Noisily
[C] Politely
[D] Quitely 
B
~
To solve "A is to B as C is to D" (A:B :: C:D) analogy questions, the primary strategy is to identify the exact relationship between A and B and then apply that same relationship to C to find D. In this example, secretly is opposite of openly; so the answer should be opposite of silently.
~
- 3
Statements: 
 No women teacher can play. 
 Some women teachers are athletes.
 Conclusions: 
 I. Male athletes can play. 
 II. Some athletes can play.
 
[A] Only conclusion I follows 
[B] Only conclusion II follows
[C] Either I or II follows 
[D] Neither I nor II follows
D
~
No women teacher can play: This is a universal negative statement about women teachers and their ability to play.
Some women teachers are athletes: This is a particular affirmative statement, meaning there is an overlap between the group "women teachers" and the group "athletes."

What we know: 
The "some women teachers" who are athletes are part of the group that "cannot play". Therefore, some athletes (specifically, those who are women teachers) cannot play.

What we don't know: 
The statements provide no information about any other group of athletes, such as male athletes or athletes who are not women teachers. It is possible that other athletes can play, or that none can, and we cannot conclude anything about them. 
~

- 11
A, P, R, X, S and Z are sitting in a row. 

S and Z are in the centre. 
A and P are at the ends. 
R is sitting to the left of A. 

Who is to the right of P ?

[A] A
[B] X
[C] S
[D] Z
B
~
Given S & Z are in the center. This can have following two possibilities:
	.... S Z ...   or ...Z S....
Given A and P are at the ends
	A...S Z...P   or P...Z S....A
Given R is to the left of A. This is only possible if A is on the rightmost side	
	P...Z S R A
Now we are left with X, who must be sitting to the right of P	
	P (X) Z S R A  
~
- 12
A, B, C, D and E are sitting on a bench. 

A is sitting next to B, 
C is sitting next to D, 
D is not sitting with E who is on the left end of the bench. 
C is on the second position from the right. 
A is to the right of B and E. 
A and C are sitting together. 

In which position A is sitting ?

[A] Between B and D
[B] Between B and C
[C] Between E and D
[D] Between C and E
B
~
Given (first two statements):  
	... B/A A/B ...D/C C/D....
Given D is not next to E & E is on the left most end.
	E...B/A A/B ...D/C C/D...
Give C is on the second position from right:
	E...B/A A/B ...C D...		(since C & D are sitting next)
Given A is on the right of B & E
	E...B A...C D....
Given A & C are next to each other. So final arrangement is
	E (B) A (C) D	
so A is sitting between B and C
~

[B] Tenzin may or may not like to read fiction.
[C] Tenzin does not like to read fiction.
[D] Tenzin does not like fashion magazines.

So nothing ca be said with confidence about Tenzin whether Tenzin likes or does not like reading fiction.
