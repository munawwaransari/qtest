In how many years, simple interest on 25000 at an  interest rate 11% p.a. would be 33000

[A] 140
[B] 130
[C] 120
[D] 110
C
~
Simple Interest Formula: S.I. = (P x t x r/100) 

Given:
	P = 25000,  r=11,   S.I. = 33000
Therefore
	     33000    = 25000 x 11/100 x t
	     
	     33000    = 25 x 11 t
	     
	     33000                  3000
	 ------------- = t    =>   ------ = t  => 120 = t
	   25 x 11                   25

- 2
Compute the interest on principal 25000 compounded every month at an interest rate of 5% p.a., for a period of 2 years.

[A] 26025
[B] 27020
[C] 27630
[D] 27624
D
~
Compound Interest Formula: CI = A - P   where  Final Amount (A) = P x (1+(r/n)/100)^{n x t}

Given:
	P = 25000      r = 5    
	t = 2 years    n = 12  (since interest is calculated every month, so 12 times in a  year) 	
Therefore
	Final Amount = P x (1+(r/n)/100)^{n x t}
		   = 25000 x (1 + (5/12)/100)^{12 x 2}
		   = 25000 x (1 + (1/240)^24)
		   = 25000 x 1.1
		   = 27623.525 => approx 27624
~
- 3
What is the difference between the compound interest and simple interest on ₹3000 at a 11% rate of interest for 2 years?
Note: the compound interest is calculated annually.

[A] 600
[B] 530
[C] 030
[D] 130
C
~
Simple interest is calculated only on the initial principal amount, resulting in linear growth.

Compound interest is calculated on the principal amount plus any accumulated interest, leading to exponential growth over time. 

Compound interest grows faster and is ideal for long-term investments, while simple interest is more beneficial for borrowers seeking predictable and lower costs on loans. 

Simple Interest Formula: S.I. = (P x t x r)/100 , 
	where 
	P is principal, t is time, and r is the rate.

Compound Interest Formula: CI = A - P  
	where  A = P x (1+(r/n)/100)^{nxt}
	or     A = P x (1+r/100)^t        when n=1
	where 
	A is the final amount, P is the principal, 
	r is the annual interest rate ; n is number of times compounded interest is calculated
	t is the number of years.

Given:
	P = 3000	r=11		t=2 years     n = 1  (annual so once in a year) 
	
	Simple Interest = S.I = (P x T x r)/ = 100 = 3000 x 2 x 10/100 = 600
	
	A = P x (1+r/100)^t = P x (1+10/100)^2 = 3000 x (1.1)^2  = 3000 x 1.21 = 3630
	Compound Interest = A - P = 3630 - 3000 = 630

	So the difference between SI and CI = CI - SI
				            = 630 - 600 = 30 
~

Note: the compound interest is calculated monthly.
Choose the best answer.

[A] 30
[B] 61
[C] 60
[D] 31
B
~
Simple interest is calculated only on the initial principal amount, resulting in linear growth.

Compound interest is calculated on the principal amount plus any accumulated interest, leading to exponential growth over time. 

Compound interest grows faster and is ideal for long-term investments, while simple interest is more beneficial for borrowers seeking predictable and lower costs on loans. 

Simple Interest Formula: S.I. = (P x t x r)/100 , 
	where 
	P is principal, t is time, and r is the rate.

Compound Interest Formula: CI = A - P  
	where  A = P x (1+(r/n)/100)^{n x t}
	or     A = P x (1+r/100)^t        when n=1
	where 
	A is the final amount, P is the principal, 
	r is the annual interest rate ; n is number of times compounded interest is calculated
	t is the number of years.

Given:
	P = 3000	r=11		t=2 years     n = 12  (every month so 12 times a year) 
	
	Simple Interest = S.I = (P x T x r)/ = 100 = 3000 x 2 x 10/100 = 600
	
	A = P x (1+(r/n)/100)^{nxt} 
	  = P x (1+(10/12)/100)^(12 x 2)
	  = 3000 x (1+1/120)^24 
	  = 3661 (approx)
	  
	Compound Interest = A - P = 3661 - 3000 = 661 (approx)

	So the difference between SI and CI = CI - SI
				            = 661 - 600 = 61 (approx)

- 5
A man borrows a sum of money at a simple interest of 6% for the first 2 years, 8% for the next 4 years, and 10% for the period beyond 6 years. If he pays a total of ₹7400 in interest at the end of 9 years, what was the principal amount he borrowed?

[A] 11000
[B] 5000 
[C] 10000
[D] 1000
C
~
Solution:
Calculate the interest for each time period and then sum them up.
Given:
	Total period = t = 9 years
	Period 1 = t1 = 2 years	  at the rate = r1 = 6%
	Period 2 = t2 = 4 years	  at the rate = r2 = 8%
	Period 3 (beyond 6 years) = t3 = (9-6) = 3 years at the rate = r3 = 10%
	Total Interest = 7400
	
	Total Interest = Simple interest of all three periods added together
            = (P x r1 x t1)/100 + (P x r2 x t2)/100 + (P x r3 x t3)/100
            = (P x 6 x 2)/100 + (P x 8 x 4)/100 + (P x 10 x 3)/100
            = 12P/100 + 32P/100 + 30P/100 = (12P+32P+30P)/100 
            = 74P/100
        since total interest is given
            74P/100 = 7400
        => P = (7400 x 100) / 74 = 100 x 100 
             = 10000    
~
- 6
A man lends a certain sum of money at simple interest. Rate of interest for first one and a half years is 10%, for next 6 months is 8%, and after that 6%, if he received Rs. 31000 as interest at the end of 4 years. What will be the principal?

[A] 10000
[B] 110000
[C] 1000
[D] 100000
D
~
Calculate the interest for each time period and then sum them up.
Given:
	Use Simple interest for calculation.
	Total period = T = 4 years
	Period 1 = t1 = 1.5 years,  		rate = r1 = 10% 
	Period 2 = t2 = 6 months = 0.5 years,   rate = r2 = 8%
	Period 3 = t3 (remaining) = 4 - (1.5+0.5) = 2 years,  rate = r1 = 6%
	Total Interest = 22000
	
	Total Interest = Simple interest of all three periods added together
            = (P x r1 x t1)/100 + (P x r2 x t2)/100 + (P x r3 x t3)/100
            = (P x 10 x 1.5)/100 + (P x 8 x 0.5)/100 + (P x 6 x 2)/100
            = 15P/100 + 4P/100 + 12P/100 = 31P/100
        since total interest is given
            31P/100 = 31100
        => P = (31000 x 100) / 31 = 1000 x 100 
             = 100000    
~
