- 1
Which nerve is the thickest nerve in the body?

[A] Femoral nerve
[B] Sciatic nerve
[C] Obtrutor nerve
[D] None of the above
B
~
The sciatic nerve is the thickest (and longest) nerve in the human body, originating in the lower back, running through the buttocks and down the back of each leg, and is a bundle of nerves that can reach up to 2 centimeters in width, connecting the spinal cord to the leg and foot.
