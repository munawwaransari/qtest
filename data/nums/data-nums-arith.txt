<b>using 2's compliment method:</b>
To subtract binary number B (subtrahend) from A (minuend) using 2's complement, find the 2's complement of B, add it to A, and interpret the final carry. If a carry occurs, discard it for a positive result; if no carry occurs, take the 2's complement of the sum and make it negative.

first number  = (00000111)<sub>2</sub>
second number = (00000110)<sub>2</sub>

2's compliment of second number: [1's compliment] + 1
    =   (001)<sub>2</sub>     [1's compliment]
      + (  1)<sub>2</sub>      plus 1
    =   (010)<sub>2</sub>

Subtraction = [first number] + [2's compliment of second number]
    
          1   <--- carry
         (111)<sub>2</sub>
         (010)<sub>2</sub>
   +------------------
       (1 001)<sub>2</sub>   
        |
        ---> extra carry bit indicates a positive result
