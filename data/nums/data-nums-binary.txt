What is the LSB (Least Significant Byte) of the 16-bit binary number 101?

[A] 1
[B] 00000101
[C] 0
[D] None of these
C
~
The 16-bit number (101)<sub>2</sub> would be (00000000 00000101)<sub>2</sub>.
So the LSB=00000000 and MSB=00000101
